Case Study

IT Process Compliance Assessment

Monitoring system activity and time logs against defined workflows, flagging deviations, and following up to reinforce process compliance.

Focus area
Process compliance
Context
Distributed teams across two project management roles
Tools
Project management systems · Time logs · SOPs · Workflow automation
Role
Project Manager

Overview

Across two project management roles, I monitored how teams followed defined processes in their project management systems, and I helped set the standards those processes were measured against.

Risk & objective

When people work in distributed teams, processes drift. Unlogged time, skipped steps, and inconsistent handling make delivery harder to trust and harder to explain. The objective was to check actual activity against the defined workflow, spot deviations early, and bring work back in line.

What I did

  • Established company-wide SOPs and governance frameworks to improve operational consistency and accountability (Repurpose House).
  • Monitored project management system activity and time logs to identify workflow deviations (Engin8).
  • Followed up on deviations to reinforce process compliance.
  • Implemented workflow automation that reduced reliance on real-time meetings and increased team productivity by 50% (Engin8).
  • Process compliance monitoring is also part of my current role at a technology advisory firm.

Controls in place

  • Documented SOPs
  • Governance frameworks
  • Activity and time-log monitoring
  • Follow-up on deviations

Result

More consistent workflows, with deviations identified and followed up, and a 50% productivity increase after workflow automation.

What this shows

I can define a standard, compare actual behavior against it, and drive corrective action. This is the shape of a compliance assessment: criteria, evidence, exceptions, and follow-up.

Skills demonstrated

  • Process Compliance Monitoring
  • SOP Documentation
  • Governance Frameworks
  • Workflow Automation
  • Follow-up on Deviations
