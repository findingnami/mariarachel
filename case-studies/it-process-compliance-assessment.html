Case Study

IT Process Compliance Assessment

Monitoring system activity and time logs against defined workflows, flagging deviations, and following up to reinforce process compliance.

Focus
Process compliance
Context
Distributed project teams
Tools
Project management systems, SOPs
Role
Project Manager

Overview

Across two project management roles, I monitored how teams followed defined processes in their project management systems, and I helped set the standards those processes were measured against.

Risk & objective

In distributed teams, processes drift. Skipped steps, inconsistent handling, and incomplete records make delivery harder to trust and harder to explain. The objective was to compare actual activity with the defined workflow, spot deviations early, and bring work back in line.

Approach

  1. Set the standard. Establish company-wide SOPs and governance frameworks that define how work should be done (Repurpose House).
  2. Decide what shows compliance. Use project management system activity and time logs as the evidence of whether the process was followed.
  3. Review the evidence. Check activity and time logs against the defined workflow (Engin8).
  4. Identify deviations. Note where actual practice differs from the standard.
  5. Follow up. Raise each deviation with the people involved and reinforce the expected process.
  6. Reduce the need for manual checks. Implement workflow automation that reduced reliance on real-time meetings and increased team productivity by 50% (Engin8).

Result

More consistent workflows, with deviations identified and followed up, and a 50% increase in team productivity after workflow automation.

Through an audit lens

A compliance assessment follows a set path: criteria, evidence, test, finding, and follow-up. I have used that path on process compliance, and I have also written the criteria in the form of SOPs. That helps me tell the difference between a process that is not followed and a process that is poorly defined, which is a distinction good audit findings make.
