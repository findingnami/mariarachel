Case Study

Financial Reconciliation & Controls

Ongoing reconciliation of vendor commissions against contracts, vendor statements, and payment records, resulting in $4,809.80 recovered from identified errors.

Focus area
Financial reconciliation
Context
21 vendors, 22 clients, and 85 contract records at a technology advisory firm
Tools
Contracts · Vendor statements · Payment records · Internal applications
Role
Operations Intelligence Manager

Overview

I perform ongoing commission reconciliation across 21 vendors, 22 clients, and 85 contract records at a technology advisory firm. Each commission is checked against the contract terms, the vendor's statement, and the payment record.

Risk & objective

Commissions are calculated from contract terms and paid by vendors on their own schedules. Calculation errors, missing or delayed payments, and gaps in supporting documents can go unnoticed without a regular check. The objective was to confirm that what was owed matches what was reported and what was paid, and to follow up on anything that does not.

What I did

  • Verified commission calculations against contractual terms, vendor statements, and payment records.
  • Investigated discrepancies, including missing or delayed payments and documentation gaps.
  • Followed up with vendors on commission items totaling $68,481.48.
  • Verified supporting documents for each identified error.
  • Recovered $4,809.80 from identified vendor commission errors.

Controls in place

  • Three-way comparison (contract, statement, payment)
  • Supporting-document verification
  • Vendor follow-up
  • Activity logs and approval workflows in the internal applications

Result

$4,809.80 recovered from vendor commission errors, and a repeatable reconciliation process with documented follow-up on open items.

What this shows

I can compare independent sources, investigate exceptions to their cause, and resolve them with evidence. This is the core of substantive testing and of financial control work.

Skills demonstrated

  • Financial Reconciliation
  • Discrepancy Investigation
  • Vendor Follow-up
  • Supporting-Document Verification
  • Substantive Testing
  • Documentation
